# Asset provenance

This file records the known provenance of non-code assets distributed in the
Apprentice repository. It does not make claims about files outside the tracked
repository.

## Observer dashboard concept

`docs/design/observer-dashboard-concept.png` was created specifically for
Apprentice during its design process. The people, sessions, metrics, traces, and
other examples visible in the image are synthetic and do not represent real
learner or customer data.

The image is distributed under the repository's Apache-2.0 license.

## Runtime assets

Apprentice does not currently distribute third-party runtime images, fonts,
icons, or other media assets. Its runtime interface is rendered from the
repository's HTML and CSS.

When adding an asset, contributors must document its source, creator, applicable
license, and any required attribution here. Do not commit assets unless their
terms are compatible with distribution in this project.
